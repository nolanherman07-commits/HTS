Step-by-step guidance

How to be the first site that pops up.

No one can honestly promise the #1 spot on Google. These are the steps Google rewards, and I walk you through every one.

  1. 1

    Claim your Google Business Profile

    Fill out every part: “Real estate agent” as your category, the towns you serve, hours, photos, and a link to your new site.

  2. 2

    Get a Google review after every closing

    Send clients a direct review link the day you close, and reply to every review. A steady flow beats one big batch.

  3. 3

    Put your towns in your words

    Titles and headings like “Homes for sale in Hickory, NC,” with a page for each town you work. I set this up when I build your site.

  4. 4

    Keep your name the same everywhere

    The same name, office address and phone number on Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Facebook and your brokerage page.

  5. 5

    Add something new every month

    New listings, closings, market updates and photos. Text them to me and they go up, so Google keeps coming back.

  6. 6

    Get local sites to link to you

    Lenders, inspectors, your chamber of commerce and local sponsorships can all link to your site. Each one is a vote for you.

  7. 7

    Be fast on phones

    Most people search from their phone. Every site I build loads fast and looks right on every screen, so this one is done for you.

  8. 8

    Share your link everywhere

    Texts after every call, your email signature and your social posts. Steady visits tell Google people want your site.
